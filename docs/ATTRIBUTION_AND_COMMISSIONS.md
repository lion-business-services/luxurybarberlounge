# Attribution and Commissions

The platform preserves the approved independent-barber policy architecture.

- Attribution: SHOP or BARBER, supported by evidence, imported roster, claim, decision, and rule version.
- Commission Basis: eligible service amount after approved exclusions.
- Calculated Amount: integer-cent calculation using basis points and line-level rounding.
- Statements: period records that may be reviewed and locked.
- Adjustments: separate immutable corrections; locked calculations are not overwritten.
- Disputes: deadline, evidence, events, decision, and audit history.
- Settlement: owner-reviewed manual status. The platform does not move money automatically.

Employment terminology such as payroll, wage, salary, paycheck, or employee must not be used for independent-barber settlement records.
