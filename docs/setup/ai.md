# AI Setup

AI is an optional assistant layer, not a dependency of booking, queue, payments, permissions, attribution, or commission.

The development concierge answers only from curated services, barbers, business information, FAQ, and policy content. A live provider must preserve the same grounding and structured-action boundaries.

Allowed public tools:

- list published services and barbers
- explain hours, location, FAQ, and policies
- recommend from approved records
- create a lead
- hand off to a person

Forbidden public actions:

- invent price or availability
- promise refunds
- expose CRM or financial records
- change bookings without validated booking tools
- alter commission or attribution
- assign roles

Admin AI drafts require review before publication or sending.
