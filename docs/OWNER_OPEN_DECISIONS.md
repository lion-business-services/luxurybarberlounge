# Owner Open Decisions

These items are intentionally not activated by default:

1. Booth rental or percentage commission structure.
2. Rent amount/period and interaction with the split.
3. Processing-fee treatment.
4. Product commission rate.
5. Whether product commission exists.
6. Membership imputed service values.
7. Package per-visit values.
8. Deposit amount.
9. No-show fee.
10. Late-cancellation fee.
11. Late-cancellation time window.
12. Confirmed start date for each Independent Barber.
13. Signed independent-contractor agreement status.
14. Final Barber roster and verified identities.
15. Final service list, durations, prices, and deposits.
16. Proposed roster-window approval.
17. Statement lock and manual settlement schedule approval.
18. Record-retention and notice approval.

Use `/admin/policies` to record decisions, effective dates, and acknowledgements. The structural classification question requires legal review and is not resolved by terminology or software.
