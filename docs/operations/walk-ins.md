# Walk-In Queue Operations

The queue supports waiting, assigned/confirmed, called, checked in, in service, completed, no-show, cancelled, and removed outcomes. Public wait times are estimates.

Assignment considers service duration, barber eligibility, scheduled appointments, breaks, client preference, current workload, location, and authorized manual priority. Staff retain final control.

Public status uses a privacy-safe token. Lobby displays must not expose full client names or phone numbers. Queue and kiosk feature flags remain off until staff training, SMS consent, capacity limits, and closed-state handling are approved.
