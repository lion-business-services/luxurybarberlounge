# Attribution

The authoritative operating policy is `LBL-Commission-Policy-v1.0`. The implementation preserves its locked defaults and separates proposed/open terms.

## Locked default

Every unresolved client-and-Barber pairing is SHOP. Walk-ins are SHOP. BARBER requires approved evidence or another approved source. Attribution is recorded against the pairing, not merely the client.

## Claim workflow

The Independent Barber submits client reference, claim type, explanation, prior-service facts, and optional evidence. Evidence is private. The owner can approve, reject, or request information with a written reason. Approval creates an auditable pairing record. Barbers cannot edit attribution directly.

## Verified pre-existing criteria

The engine supports paid prior service, service before Barber start, approved lookback, service outside the Lounge, and documentary evidence. The proposed 14-day roster window is configurable and is not enforced until approved.

## Audit

Record source, campaign/referral, evidence, reviewer, reason, rule version, effective date, lock state, and history. Later corrections use a new decision/Adjustment rather than rewriting a locked record.
