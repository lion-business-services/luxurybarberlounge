# Roles and Permissions

## Client

Own profile, appointments, queue records, membership, grooming preferences, notifications, consent, feedback, account, export/deletion requests.

## Independent Barber

Assigned operational appointments and authorized client service details, own queue/schedule/portfolio/performance, imported roster, evidence claims, calculated amounts, statements, adjustments, disputes, and policy acknowledgements. Cannot edit attribution, calculations, locked lines, or historical records.

## Receptionist

Today’s operational schedule, client lookup/create, check-in, walk-in creation, queue, assignment/reassignment, contact actions, kiosk control, shop status. No owner-only rule, credential, or commission configuration.

## Manager

Authorized business/location operations, scheduling, services, queue, clients, limited reports, and delegated review tasks.

## Owner

Business administration, policies, role assignment, integrations, reconciliation, statements, content, audit, security, and owner approvals.

## Super administrator

Reserved for exceptional platform operations. Do not create casually.

Permissions are enforced by server role checks and RLS, not by menu visibility.
