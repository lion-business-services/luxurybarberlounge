# AI Features

AI is provider-neutral and feature-flagged.

Public concierge may explain approved services, verified prices, hours, directions, policies, and booking paths; recommend from approved records; capture a lead; respond in English/Spanish; and escalate to a person.

Portal assistants may summarize operations, feedback, performance, queue patterns, Statements, or integrity flags and draft content or follow-ups.

AI never invents prices, availability, services, bookings, client facts, policies, refunds, or commission decisions. It is not the final authority for queue assignment, attribution, calculation, settlement, permissions, or owner approval. The deterministic approved-content fallback works without an AI credential.
