# Motion and Responsiveness

The approved cinematic identity is preserved through a centralized adaptive-motion engine rather than a desktop-only effect pile.

## Tiers

- Full cinematic: capable desktop, richest video/depth, restrained particles and pointer response.
- Balanced: laptops, same sequence with fewer concurrent effects and lower media cost.
- Touch optimized: tablet/mobile, same story and imagery with shorter vertical motion, touch-safe controls, and native scrolling.
- Low power: still/video fallback, minimal depth and filters.
- Reduced motion: complete static luxury compositions with all content and actions.

Signals include reduced-motion preference, pointer/touch, viewport, memory/concurrency when available, connection hints, visibility, and media support. Unknown signals choose conservative behavior.

## Mobile screenshot repairs

- compact stable header
- mobile-specific hero video/poster
- intentional grand-opening badge wrapping
- responsive title/subtitle measure
- stacked primary/secondary CTAs
- concierge collapsed and delayed to prevent collision
- safe-area padding above sticky bottom navigation
- shortened sticky hero travel
- no horizontal overflow or hidden tap targets

## Ownership

Motion handles component/scroll mapping. One Lenis owner exists only where capability rules allow it. Portals do not load cinematic scrolling, cursor, particles, or homepage media. Offscreen video and decorative work pause. Essential content never depends on animation.
