# Statements and Disputes

Statements are weekly, itemized, versioned records. Each calculation line includes transaction reference, service, basis components, tips, attribution/evidence reference, rule version/effective date, rate, Calculated Amount, status, author, and timestamp.

Independent Barbers can view, print/PDF, export CSV, and dispute eligible lines. Dispute types are attribution, arithmetic, or omission. The owner records a written decision and reason. Policy percentages are not changed through line disputes.

Locked lines are immutable. Corrections create Adjustments. The platform calculates and reports; manual settlement remains an owner action until a future approved payout integration exists.
