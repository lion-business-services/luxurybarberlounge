# Independent Barber Portal

Terminology follows the operating policy: Calculated Amount, settlement, Statement, Commission Basis, Adjustment, and Independent Barber. Do not use payroll, wages, salary, paycheck, employee, or hours worked.

The workspace includes Today, calendar, appointments, clients, queue/Who’s Next, attribution claims, imported roster, evidence upload, portfolio, performance, Calculated Amounts, weekly Statements, Adjustments, disputes, schedule requests, time off, profile, notifications, and policy acknowledgement.

Independent Barbers can submit evidence and disputes. They cannot directly alter attribution, calculations, statement locks, policy percentages, or historical transaction records.

## Policy acknowledgement

`/barber/resources` separates owner-review, approved, and published policy versions. An Independent Barber may acknowledge only an approved or published version. The acknowledgement stores the version, text, signature name, timestamp, and hashed request metadata.
