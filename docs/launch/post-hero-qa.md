# Post-Hero QA Checklist

- Confirm the hero is visually unchanged on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Confirm the threshold begins directly after the hero with no blank gap.
- Verify the threshold video is muted, inline, looping, and pauses offscreen.
- Verify reduced-motion uses the poster and static scenes.
- Test the comparison slider with keyboard arrows and touch.
- Test all service, barber, visit, phone, directions, and booking links.
- Check 320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, and 1920 widths.
- Confirm no horizontal overflow and no sticky section overlaps the footer.
- Confirm generated signage is cropped or visually subordinate.
- Confirm the official logo is used for prominent brand placement.
