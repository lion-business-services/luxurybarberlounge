# Content Replacement Checklist

Owner confirmation is required before unrestricted production launch for:

- Final public display name and legal-name usage
- Business hours and holiday hours
- Parking and accessibility details
- Social profile URLs and Google Business Profile URL
- All service names, prices, duration, deposits, taxes, add-ons, and eligibility
- Real barber names, portraits, biographies, languages, specialties, schedules, credentials, social links, and Square team IDs
- Membership pricing, included services, usage, rollover, pause, cancellation, and billing mapping
- Package inclusions and event terms
- Booking, cancellation, late arrival, no-show, deposit, refund, membership, privacy, SMS, cookie, and accessibility policies
- Payment methods and gift-card status
- Real, permissioned gallery and review content

Temporary records include a `demo` or content-status marker. Do not remove those markers merely to make a dashboard appear complete.
